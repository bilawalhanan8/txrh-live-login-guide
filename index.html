TXRH Live Login Guide

TXRH Live (TXRHLive) is the Texas Roadhouse employee portal for Roadies. This guide explains how to log in, view your paystub, and check your work schedule. For the complete guide, visit TXRH Live.

TXRH Live

What Is TXRH Live?

TXRH Live is the secure online employee portal used by Texas Roadhouse, Bubba's 33, and Jaggers team members. After signing in, employees can check schedules, download pay stubs and W-2 forms, update direct deposit details, view benefits, and complete training.

How to Log In to TXRH Live

Have your employee username and password ready, plus your phone or authenticator app if multi-factor authentication is enabled.

  1. Go to the official portal at txrhlive.com.
  2. Enter your employee email address on the sign-in screen.
  3. Enter your password. Passwords are case-sensitive, so check Caps Lock first.
  4. Complete multi-factor authentication if asked, by approving the prompt on your phone or entering the code.
  5. You are now on your employee dashboard, where you can open payroll, schedules, W-2s, and benefits.

New employees receive account details during onboarding at their store. If you have never signed in before, ask your store manager for help with first-time setup.

Forgot Your TXRH Live Password?

Do not keep guessing, because repeated failed attempts can lock your account. Use the official self-service reset tool at sspr.txrh.com: enter your employee username, choose how to receive your verification code, enter the code, and set a new password. Then sign back in at txrhlive.com with the new password.

How to View Your TXRH Live Paystub

  1. Log in to TXRH Live using the steps above.
  2. Open the payroll or pay section from your employee dashboard.
  3. Choose the pay period you want to review.
  4. View your paystub online, or download or print it for your records.

Your paystub shows gross pay, hours worked, taxes, deductions, and net pay. If a paystub is missing or looks wrong, contact your store manager or payroll support. Former employees may retain limited access to final pay stubs and W-2 forms during tax season.

How to View Your TXRH Live Schedule

  1. Log in to TXRH Live.
  2. Open the schedule section from your dashboard.
  3. Check your shifts for the current and upcoming weeks.
  4. Use the same section to submit time-off requests or check availability, where your location supports it.

TXRH Live works on mobile browsers, so you can check your schedule on your phone. Keep the Microsoft Authenticator app available for verification, and sign out after you finish on a shared device.

TXRH Live Login Not Working?

  • Wrong password: check Caps Lock and retype slowly.
  • Page will not load or keeps looping: clear your browser cache and cookies, or try a private or incognito window.
  • Code not arriving: check your signal and spam folder, and confirm the phone number on your account is correct.
  • Still locked out: contact your store manager or official support for help with your account.

Stay Safe

Sign in only at the official portal, txrhlive.com, and reset passwords only via sspr.txrh.com. A real TXRH Live sign-in will never ask for your bank details, full Social Security number, or payment. Never share your password with anyone.
